instar2lox manages INSTAR Full HD cameras from LoxBerry. Tested on INSTAR Full HD 9 series (IN-9008 Full HD). Alarms are received via MQTT (LoxBerry broker) and sent to Loxone via UDP.

  • Cameras: status, model, firmware, WebUI version, SD card, reboot plan, last alarm. Poll, reboot or edit single cameras.
  • Reboot plan: per camera daily, weekly or monthly; executed by the plugin cron (once per minute check).
  • Firmware: a camera is outdated when its version is lower than the target version set for its model in Settings, or - without a target - lower than the newest version found on your other cameras of the same model.
  • Loxone: UDP is sent only on change. 0 always means OK: instar2lox.<id>.offline, .outdated, .alarm, plus offline_count and outdated_count.
  • Alarms: cameras publish instar/<MAC>/status/alarm/triggered to the LoxBerry broker. The MQTT tab can write the broker settings into each camera.
